Make a 60-second animated explainer ("advertisement") video for a healthcare software product called **AI at 70**. Follow the story, narration, visual style and on-screen text below exactly. Every person, note and number in it is made up.

## The product (what the video must show accurately)
AI at 70 is a clinician-facing web page for primary care. It takes up to three inputs from an older patient's visit:
1. **Check-in**: a simple questionnaire the patient answers when they arrive for the appointment (a few taps: visit type, what makes the care plan hard, who may receive the summary).
2. **Conversation** (optional): the visit can be voice-recorded and turned into a written transcript right away. It helps, but it can be skipped.
3. **Clinician note**: the doctor types, uploads, or **snaps a photo of their handwritten notes**. The text is read from the photo.

It then writes a one-page visit summary in plain words at a 5th-grade reading level. The page shows the patient's summary on the left and a clinician checklist on the right, with a "Select all" button. The doctor ticks every item, approves, and chooses an output:
- a large-print **printout**,
- an **email** to the patient or authorized family,
- a **phone call** read by a warm, natural AI voice.

Each output is also available in Spanish, Mandarin, Portuguese and Arabic. Nothing is sent before the doctor approves it.

App look: a clean white and soft-teal medical web app. Rounded cards, big friendly type, and a step bar reading "Check-in → Conversation → Clinician note → Review & send". The landing page is minimal: the headline "Visit summaries your patients can understand.", one line ("Plain words, checked by you, sent as a printout, email or phone call."), the four steps as pills, and the buttons "Start a visit" and "Patient check-in form". The logo text is "AI at 70".

## Characters (keep them identical in every scene)
Draw them with ordinary human anatomy: a head about 1/7 of the body's height, a normal neck, shoulders, arms with elbows reaching to the hips, and full-length legs with knees. No big heads or chibi proportions.
- **Maria Rodriguez**, 78. Gray hair in a bun, rectangular glasses, a mauve sweater, a slate-blue skirt. She comes to the appointment alone.
- **Dr. Lee**, Maria's primary care doctor. Short black hair, a knee-length white coat over a light-blue shirt, a stethoscope. Dr. Lee is the one who introduces AI at 70.
- **Sofia Rodriguez**, Maria's adult daughter. Dark hair in a ponytail, a teal top, dark trousers. She appears only at home.

## Style: modern flat illustration
- Clean, contemporary flat vector style: no outlines, soft solid color fields, large soft background circles, gentle gradients (window skies), soft drop shadows under cards and a faint floor shadow under people.
- Rounded white cards (radius ~20px) with soft shadows for on-screen information; speech bubbles are white with a small rounded tail.
- Palette (from the app): ink #12303d, blue #1c5d84, teal #38b6a9, green #1d7a55, sky #35a5e0, amber #f2b33d, coral #ee7b62, soft backgrounds #e8f3f0 (clinic), #edf3f8 (exam room), #f6efe6 (home).
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (700–800) for everything; Caveat (handwriting) for Dr. Lee's notes.
- Each scene has a step chip in the top-left (a teal number circle + label: "1 Check-in", "2 The visit", "3 AI at 70", "4 Going home", "5 At home") and a caption in a white pill with a teal dot, centered at the bottom.
- App screen recordings play inside a white browser window (three colored dots in its top bar) on a soft teal backdrop, with the caption below the window.
- 1280×720 (16:9), 30 fps. Simple motion only: slide-ins, pop-ins, gentle bobbing, check marks ticking, handwriting lines fading in, sound waves for the phone.

## How it should feel (structure from real 60-second ads)
- **Hook → problem → turn → how it works → payoff → closing line.** Spend about a quarter of the time on the problem so the solution means something, then show the product, then answer the problem in the payoff ("left guessing" → "nobody's guessing").
- **About 145 spoken words for 60 seconds.** Leave room for the pictures; don't fill every second with voice.
- **One continuous voiceover, not separate announcements.** Short, spoken sentences; small connecting words ("Now," "Then," "and"); a line can run on into the next scene ("…approves it, / and Maria leaves with it…"). Real pauses after full sentences (0.5–0.9 s), short ones where a sentence carries on (0.15–0.2 s). Never cut a word off: fade only the music at the end.
- **Crossfade every cut** (about 0.35 s); start each scene a beat (0.25 s) before its line.

## Audio
- **Narrator:** a warm, natural female voice (Kokoro af_bella, speed ~1.06), conversational, not announcer-like.
- **Say "AI" as the two letters "ay-eye"** (/eɪ aɪ/), never "uh-eye". For text-to-speech, write "AI" (not "A I") and "Doctor Lee" (not "Dr. Lee", which some voices read with a stop).
- **Music:** a soft synth pad: a low minor chord for "today", easing into a warm major chord at the turn; it fades out under the last line.
- **Phone call (scene 12):** the app's own phone voice (Kokoro af_heart, speed 0.95) with a light telephone EQ: "Blood pressure medicine, called Amlodipine." (short breath) "Take 1 pill, 5 milligrams, each day." Each line appears on screen as it is spoken.

## Rules
- Show the problem first (today), then one appointment from arrival to home with AI at 70, with one set of text throughout. The jargon in scene 2 and the shorthand in scene 6 must match the note, and every app screen must match the content above.
- Never show a medicine, dose or instruction that isn't in the note.
- Show the app's step order exactly as Check-in → Conversation → Clinician note → Review & send.
- AI at 70 is introduced by the physician (Dr. Lee) during the visit, as the doctor's tool for turning notes into a usable summary.
- The conversation recording is shown as optional.
- Keep the tone respectful toward older adults: warm, never childish or mocking. The "today" scenes show confusion, not foolishness.
- Use no real brands, hospitals or people.
