Make a 60-second animated explainer ("advertisement") video for a healthcare software product called **AI at 70**. Follow the story, narration, visual style and on-screen text below exactly. Every person, note and number in it is made up.

## The product (what the video must show accurately)
AI at 70 is a clinician-facing web page for primary care. It takes up to three inputs from an older patient's visit:
1. **Check-in**: a short questionnaire the patient fills out before the visit, in their own words. It also records who may receive the summary.
2. **Conversation**: the visit is voice-recorded and turned into a written transcript right away. This step can be skipped.
3. **Clinician note**: the doctor types, uploads, or **snaps a photo of their handwritten notes**. The text is read from the photo.

It then writes a one-page visit summary in plain words at a 5th-grade reading level. The page shows the patient's summary on the left and a clinician checklist on the right, with a "Select all" button. The doctor ticks every item, approves, and chooses an output:
- a large-print **printout**,
- an **email** to the patient or authorized family,
- a **phone call** read by a warm, natural AI voice.

Each output is also available in Spanish, Mandarin, Portuguese and Arabic. Nothing is sent before the doctor approves it.

App look: a clean white and soft-teal medical web app. Rounded cards, big friendly type, and a step bar reading "Check-in → Conversation → Clinician note → Review & send". The logo text is "AI at 70".

## Characters (keep them identical in every scene)
- **Maria Rodriguez**, 78. Short gray hair, glasses, a cardigan. She has type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure, and her legs have been swollen for two weeks.
- **Sofia Rodriguez**, her adult daughter. Dark hair in a ponytail, casual clothes.
- **Dr. Lee**, Maria's primary care doctor. White coat, stethoscope.

## Style
- Flat 2D vector cartoon with friendly rounded shapes and soft shadows.
- Fonts: Nunito / Nunito Sans.
- 1280×720 (16:9), 30 fps.
- Palette: ink #12303d, blue #1c5d84, teal #38b6a9, green #1d7a55, amber #f2b33d, paper #fffdf6.
- The first three scenes carry a gray "TODAY" chip in the top-left and slightly desaturated colors. All later scenes carry a teal "WITH AI AT 70" chip and full color.
- Each scene has a short caption in a dark rounded pill, centered at the bottom.
- Simple motion only: pop-in speech bubbles, slide-ins, gentle bobbing, and floating gray "?" marks for confusion.

## Audio
- **Narrator:** a warm, friendly American male voice, at a slightly brisk pace.
- **Music:** a soft synth pad under the narration. A minor chord during "today" (scenes 1–3), changing to a brighter major chord from scene 4 on.
- **Phone call (scene 10):** a warm female AI voice with a light telephone EQ, at a slightly slow pace. She says: "Blood pressure medicine, called Amlodipine. Take 1 pill, 5 milligrams, each day."
