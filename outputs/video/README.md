## The story

It follows one made-up patient throughout: Maria Rodriguez, 78, her daughter Sofia, and Dr. Lee.

1. **The problem today:** a check-up full of jargon, then a confused evening at home, where Sofia is "left guessing".
2. **The turn:** "Now, the same visit, with AI at 70."
3. **How it works:**
   - a simple check-in
   - Dr. Lee types up notes
   - Dr. Lee introduces AI at 70 (inputs → outputs)
   - recording the conversation adds helpful detail
   - the typed notes are uploaded
   - Dr. Lee approves the summary
   - translation into Spanish, Mandarin, Portuguese or Arabic, with Spanish shown
   - Maria leaves with the printout
4. **At home:** Sofia gets the email and a friendly phone call. "This time, nobody's guessing."
5. **Close:** "AI at 70. Quick. Reliable. Understandable."
