# outputs/presentation/

[`AI_at_70.pptx`](AI_at_70.pptx) is a 4-slide presentation in the app's colors and logo. Its pictures come from the demo video and the app itself.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title: AI at 70, the project statement ("In our project, a person always stays in charge"), the authors, and Maria and Sofia at home with every output |
| 2 | **Who are we building this for, and why do they need it?** Elderly patients who have difficulty retaining medical information and guidance. Shown with the video's "at the visit" and "at home" scenes. |
| 3 | **What could go wrong, and what would we do about it?** AI can make mistakes, so physicians always verify the final output. Shown with the app's real review and approval screen. |
| 4 | **How does this help people rather than decide for them?** There is no change in medical guidance, only simplification. Shown with the physician's note next to what the patient reads, and Dr. Lee explaining what goes in and what comes out. |

Each slide has speaker notes. The author names are first names; edit `AUTHORS` in [`scripts/presentation/make_presentation.py`](../../scripts/presentation/make_presentation.py) to change them, then rebuild (see [scripts/presentation/README.md](../../scripts/presentation/README.md)).
